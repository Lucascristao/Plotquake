# ITT — visual credits

Complete attribution for the selected original photographs, archive excerpts, official document pages and original explanatory artwork. Prepared October 17, 2026.

## Cover and typography

The dedicated CIA CONNECTION cover is an original editorial composition, not a historical photograph of an ITT/CIA transaction. Its preserved telephone reference is [Western Electric Model 500](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WE500dialphone.jpg), by ProhibitOnions, public domain. The desk, folder, lighting and composition are editorial; the model is not attributed to ITT. The headline is typeset from the unmodified [Anton Regular font by Vernon Adams](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Anton), under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/). The font license is preserved with the source files.

- No reusable licensed Chile 1970 election or 1973 ITT hearing motion footage was secured. Bell System footage is identified only as American telephone-industry context; historical Chile passages use authentic photographs and original documents.
- No verified reusable Harold Geneen or William Broe portrait was secured. Their actions are shown through genuine documentary records and labeled explanatory diagrams, without invented faces.
